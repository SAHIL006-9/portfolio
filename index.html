About

Sahil is a Cloud & DevOps Engineer who likes systems that run themselves — infrastructure that provisions, deploys, and monitors without someone babysitting it. He's an AWS Certified Solutions Architect who spent the past year at CETPA Infotech turning manual infrastructure setup into Terraform and CloudFormation, and building CI/CD pipelines with Jenkins that cut release time by nearly a third.

His personal projects push the same idea further: Kubernetes clusters on EKS that autoscale under simulated traffic spikes, a CI/CD pipeline that blocks vulnerable container images before they ship, and Grafana dashboards that catch problems before anyone has to go looking.

He graduated in Electronics and Communication Engineering in 2025 and is now looking for an entry-level DevOps Engineer or Cloud Engineer role where he can keep building infrastructure that's automated, observable, and easy to trust.
